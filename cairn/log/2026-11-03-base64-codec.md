# Basic proxy authorization runs on base64-codec

The base64 crate gave way to base64-codec 0.1, as the family decided on 2026-11-03. `HttpCredentials::header_value` in src/http/connect.rs encodes the `user:pass` pair through `Base64Codec::default().encode_vec`, straight after the `Basic ` prefix. Encoding is the crate's only base64 use, so no error or type changes and the release can stay 0.1.x; the minimum Rust version rises to 1.89 with the codec. This drops base64 0.23 from the trees of pimalaya-stream and of every client above it (io-smtp, io-imap, io-http) once released.

Capabilities moved: packaging (the `http` feature pulls base64-codec).

Verified: `request_includes_basic_auth` gains a padded pair (`a:b1`, `YTpiMQ==`); `cargo clippy --all-targets --all-features` clean, 25 unit tests passed, clippy clean on `--no-default-features` alone and with `http` and `socks5,client`; `cargo tree -i base64 --all-features` finds no base64.
